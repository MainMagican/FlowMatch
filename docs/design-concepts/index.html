FlowMatch — 5 minimalist landing page concepts

Click any card to open the full concept in a new view.

1

Mono Editorial

Black & white, no cards or gradients — big type, hairline rules, one accent color used sparingly.

 2

Swiss Grid

Strict grid, thin borders, flat squared corners — structured and functional, no shadows/glow.

 3

Soft Neutral

Warm light-gray canvas, soft rounded card, one subtle shadow — calm and approachable.

 4

Dark Minimal

Near-black background, single accent line, no background glow blobs — quiet and focused.

 5

Single-Column Focus

Everything centered in one narrow column; the 4 feature blurbs collapse under a "See how it works" toggle.
